Activities Completed

- Discussed Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
- Revisited the Principle of Least Privilege.
- Reviewed network segmentation and its role in restricting network communication.

Key Takeaways

Authentication verifies identity, while authorization determines access permissions. RBAC assigns permissions according to job roles, and MFA provides additional protection when passwords are compromised.
