Key Takeaways

- Authentication verifies identity, while authorization determines what a user can access.
- MFA adds a layer of protection by requiring multiple verification factors.
- Access-control models manage permissions using roles, attributes, or defined rules.
- The principle of least privilege limits users to the permissions they need.
- Revoking active sessions and restricting compromised accounts can help stop unauthorized access.
