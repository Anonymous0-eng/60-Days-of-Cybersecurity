Day 28 – Weekly Review and SOC Investigation

Date: November 15, 2026
Journey: 60 Days of Cybersecurity

Focus

SOC Investigation, Incident Containment, and Breach Impact Assessment.

Objective

To strengthen my ability to investigate suspicious login activity, contain compromised accounts, and assess the impact of a potential data breach.

Activities Completed

- Investigated suspicious login activity involving multiple failed attempts followed by an unfamiliar successful login.
- Identified useful evidence, including login timestamps, source IP addresses, file activity, and the employee's confirmation.
- Discussed containment actions such as revoking active sessions, disabling compromised accounts, isolating affected devices, and escalating incidents.
- Explored how to determine the scope of a breach by examining accessed, downloaded, modified, or deleted files.
- Considered data sensitivity, duration of unauthorized access, and possible exposure of information outside the organization.

Key Takeaways

- Containment helps prevent further unauthorized access and limit damage.
- Isolating compromised devices can help prevent lateral movement.
- Understanding the sensitivity of affected data is essential when assessing breach impact.
- Preserving logs and investigating the attacker's activities helps establish the full scope of an incident.

Reflection

Today's exercise helped me think more systematically about SOC investigations. I learned that identifying suspicious activity is only the beginning; I must also contain the threat, preserve evidence, and understand the extent of the damage.

Status

Completed – Weekly review and scenario-based SOC investigation.
