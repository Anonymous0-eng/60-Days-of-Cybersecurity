Day 31 – Endpoint Security and Threat Monitoring

Date: November 18, 2026
Journey: 60 Days of Cybersecurity

Focus

Endpoint Security, Threat Detection, and Monitoring.

Objective

To strengthen my understanding of endpoint protection mechanisms and how security analysts identify and investigate suspicious endpoint activities.

Activities Completed

- Studied endpoint security and its importance in protecting connected devices.
- Explored antivirus, Endpoint Protection Platforms (EPP), Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR), host-based firewalls, and patch management.
- Reviewed common endpoint threats, including malware, ransomware, Trojans, spyware, and worms.
- Studied endpoint monitoring, process behaviour, and suspicious activities.
- Examined how SOC analysts interpret endpoint alerts and investigate potentially malicious processes.
- Learned about true positives, false positives, true negatives, and false negatives.

Key Takeaways

- Endpoint security protects devices against malware, unauthorized access, and other threats.
- EDR provides visibility into endpoint activities and supports threat investigation and response.
- Legitimate applications and administrative tools can be abused by attackers.
- Security alerts require investigation before they can be classified as genuine incidents.
- Understanding normal endpoint behaviour helps analysts identify suspicious activity.

Reflection

Today's structured lessons helped me understand endpoint security beyond simply identifying malware. I gained a clearer understanding of how endpoint protection tools work, what they monitor, and how SOC analysts investigate suspicious behaviour.
