Day 29 – Phishing Investigation and Account Compromise

Date: November 16, 2026
Journey: 60 Days of Cybersecurity

Focus

Phishing Detection, Account Containment, and Post-Compromise Investigation.

Objective

To develop my ability to identify phishing attempts, respond to compromised credentials, and investigate unauthorized account activity.

Activities Completed

- Identified phishing indicators, including suspicious sender domains, grammatical errors, inconsistent branding, suspicious links, and urgency.
- Explored immediate responses to compromised credentials, including account suspension, session revocation, password resets, and MFA verification.
- Discussed when device isolation may be necessary to prevent further compromise.
- Examined authentication and email logs to identify unauthorized activities.
- Identified suspicious email forwarding rules, unauthorized account changes, and potential data exposure as areas for investigation.

Key Takeaways

- Phishing can expose organizational credentials and provide attackers with unauthorized access.
- Account containment and device isolation address different security risks.
- Reviewing logs helps establish what an attacker did after gaining access.
- Unauthorized email forwarding rules can allow attackers to maintain access to sensitive communications.

Reflection

Today's exercise strengthened my understanding of phishing investigations and account compromise. I learned that responding to stolen credentials involves more than changing a password; it also requires revoking existing sessions, investigating account activity, and checking for persistence mechanisms.

Status

Completed – Scenario-based phishing investigation and account compromise exercise.
