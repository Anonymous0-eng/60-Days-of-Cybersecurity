Focus

SOC investigation, account compromise and data breach response.

Objective

Apply previously learned cybersecurity concepts to investigate unauthorized account access and recommend appropriate security measures.

Activities Completed

- Analysed suspicious login attempts and identified indicators of possible account compromise.
- Identified account suspension and session revocation as containment measures.
- Reviewed security logs to establish the scope of unauthorized activity.
- Examined the implications of confidential data being accessed and downloaded.
- Recommended least privilege, MFA and network segmentation as preventive controls.

Key Takeaways

Effective security investigation involves identifying suspicious activity, containing threats, analysing evidence and determining the impact of an incident. Preventive measures such as MFA, least privilege, network segmentation and continuous monitoring help reduce security risks.
