My reasoning was that responding to a potential threat requires controlling the risk before it causes further damage.

I also recognised the importance of preserving security logs rather than deleting them, since they may contain evidence needed to investigate the incident.

3. Incident Response Phases Reviewed

I reinforced my understanding of three important incident-response phases:

- Containment: Limiting the threat to prevent further damage or spread.
- Eradication: Removing the threat and addressing its cause.
- Recovery: Restoring affected systems to normal, secure operation.

I also recognised the importance of identification and investigation in determining what happened and guiding the appropriate response.

The response taken during an actual incident should follow the organisation's procedures and account for the potential impact on affected systems.
