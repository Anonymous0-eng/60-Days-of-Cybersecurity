Day 33 – Cybersecurity Revision and Incident Documentation

Date: November 20, 2026
Journey: 60 Days of Cybersecurity

Focus

SOC Incident Investigation, Malware Response, and Security Documentation.

Objective

Apply previously learned cybersecurity concepts to investigate a suspected malware infection, assess its impact, and recommend preventive measures.

Activities Completed

- Analysed an EDR alert involving Microsoft Word launching PowerShell after an email attachment was opened.
- Identified relevant evidence sources, including email, network, user activity, process, and file activity logs.
- Examined how to determine whether sensitive files were accessed, modified, or transferred.
- Reviewed containment measures, including endpoint isolation, session revocation, and blocking malicious network communication.
- Identified preventive controls such as MFA, least privilege, access control, email filtering, EDR, network segmentation, and security awareness training.

Key Takeaways

- Process activity can reveal suspicious behaviour and potential malware execution.
- Multiple log sources are necessary to establish an incident timeline.
- File modification does not automatically establish data exfiltration.
- Effective incident response requires containment, eradication, and recovery.
- Preventive security controls reduce the likelihood and impact of future attacks.

Reflection

This exercise strengthened my understanding of SOC investigations and helped me connect endpoint monitoring, evidence collection, incident response, and preventive security measures.
